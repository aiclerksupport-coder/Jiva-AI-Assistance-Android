# Legal notice review needed

`TERMS_OF_USE.md` and `DISCLAIMER.md` are draft informational materials, not legal advice and not a guarantee of immunity from claims. The standard MIT `LICENSE` grants software-use rights and includes its own no-warranty/liability language. Neither the README nor an in-app acknowledgement can waive rights or liabilities that applicable law makes non-waivable.

Before distributing binaries, collecting data, publishing on app stores, or accepting contributions at scale, ask qualified counsel to review copyright ownership, privacy notices, consumer-protection requirements, AI disclosures, third-party provider terms, jurisdiction, and contributor licensing. Do not market the draft as a complete enforceable Terms of Service.
